证明
$$
\mathbf{a} \cdot \mathbf{b} = |\mathbf{a}| |\mathbf{b}| \cos \theta = x_a x_b + y_a y_b
$$
![Pasted image 20250309100217](https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/breezyfrost/image-host/20250309100255403.png)
根据向量的加法:
$$
\begin{align}
\mathbf{a} \cdot \mathbf{b} & = (\vec{a_x} + \vec{a_y}) \cdot (\vec{b_x} + \vec{b_y})  \\
& = \vec{a_x} \cdot \vec{b_x} + \vec{a_x} \cdot \vec{b_y} + \vec{a_y} \cdot \vec{b_x} + \vec{a_y} \cdot \vec{b_y} \\
& = |a_x|  |b_x| \cos 0^\circ + |a_x| |b_y| \cos \frac{\pi}{2} + |a_y|  |b_x| \cos \frac{\pi}{2} + |a_y| |b_y| \cos 0^\circ \\
& = |a_x|  |b_x| + |a_y| |b_y| \\
& = x_a x_b + y_a y_b
\end{align}
$$
