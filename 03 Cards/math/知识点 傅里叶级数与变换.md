## 三角函数系的正交性
集合 $\{\sin 0x=0, \cos 0x=1, \sin x, \cos x, \sin 2x, \cos 2x, \cdots \}$, 在区间 $[- \pi, \pi]$ 上是正交函数系.
即当 $m \ne n$ 时, 以下等式成立: 
$$
\begin{align}
\int_{-\pi}^{\pi} \sin(nx) \cos(mx) \, dx = 0 \\
\int_{-\pi}^{\pi} \sin(nx) \sin(mx) \, dx = 0 \\
\int_{-\pi}^{\pi} \cos(nx) \cos(mx) \, dx = 0 \\
\end{align}
$$
### 证明 $\int_{-\pi}^{\pi} \sin(nx) \cos(mx) \, dx = 0$
$$
\begin{align}
& \int_{-\pi}^{\pi} \sin(nx) \cos(mx) \, dx \\
=& \int_{-\pi}^{\pi} \frac{1}{2} [\sin(n+m) + \sin(n-m)] \, dx \\
=& \frac{1}{2} [\int_{-\pi}^{\pi} \sin(n+m) \, dx + \int_{-\pi}^{\pi} \sin(n-m) \, dx ] \\
=& \frac{1}{2} [ -\frac{1}{n + m} \cos(n + m)x \Big|_{-\pi}^{\pi} - \frac{1}{n + m} \cos(n + m)x \Big|_{-\pi}^{\pi} ] \\
=& 0
\end{align}
$$
可以看出, 该公式对 $n = m$ 时也成立

### 证明 $\int_{-\pi}^{\pi} \sin(nx) \sin(mx) \, dx = 0$
$$
\begin{align}
& \int_{-\pi}^{\pi} \sin(nx) \sin(mx) \, dx \\
=& \int_{-\pi}^{\pi} \frac{1}{2} [\cos(n+m) - \cos(n-m)] \, dx \\
=& \frac{1}{2} [\int_{-\pi}^{\pi} \cos(n+m) \, dx - \int_{-\pi}^{\pi} \cos(n-m) \, dx ] \\
=& \frac{1}{2} [ \frac{1}{n + m} \sin(n + m)x \Big|_{-\pi}^{\pi} - \frac{1}{n - m} \sin(n - m)x \Big|_{-\pi}^{\pi} ] \\
=& 0
\end{align}
$$

### 证明 $\int_{-\pi}^{\pi} \cos(nx) \cos(mx) \, dx = 0$
$$
\begin{align}
& \int_{-\pi}^{\pi} \cos(nx) \cos(mx) \, dx \\
=& \int_{-\pi}^{\pi} \frac{1}{2} [\cos(n+m) + \cos(n-m)] \, dx \\
=& \frac{1}{2} [\int_{-\pi}^{\pi} \cos(n+m) \, dx + \int_{-\pi}^{\pi} \cos(n-m) \, dx ] \\
=& \frac{1}{2} [ \frac{1}{n + m} \sin(n + m)x \Big|_{-\pi}^{\pi} + \frac{1}{n - m} \sin(n - m)x \Big|_{-\pi}^{\pi} ] \\
=& 0
\end{align}
$$
## 傅里叶级数公式系数的计算
假设傅里叶级数公式成立, 即: 
$$
f(x) = \frac{a_0}{2} + \sum_{n=1}^{\infty} \left[ a_n \cos(nx) + b_n \sin(nx) \right]
$$
### 计算 $a_0$
对等式左右两边同时在区间 $[-\pi, \pi]$ 求积分:
$$
\begin{align}
\int_{-\pi}^{\pi} f(x) \, dx &= \int_{-\pi}^{\pi} \frac{a_0}{2} \, dx + \sum_{n=1}^{\infty} \int_{-\pi}^{\pi} [ a_n \cos(nx) + b_n \sin(nx)] \, dx \\
&= \frac{a_0}{2}x \Big|_{-\pi}^{\pi} + 0\\
&= \pi a_0
\end{align}
$$
解得: $a_0 = \frac{1}{\pi} \int_{-\pi}^{\pi} f(x) \, dx$.

### 计算 $a_k, k \in [1, \dots , n]$ 
对等式两边同时乘以 $\cos (kx)$,并在区间 $[-\pi, \pi]$ 求积分:
$$
\begin{align}
& \int_{-\pi}^{\pi} f(x)\cos(kx) \, dx \\ 
=& \int_{-\pi}^{\pi} \frac{a_0}{2} \cos(kx) \, dx + \sum_{n=1}^{\infty} \int_{-\pi}^{\pi} [ a_n \cos(nx)\cos(kx) + b_n \sin(nx)\cos(kx)] \, dx \\
=& 0 + \sum_{n=1}^{\infty} \int_{-\pi}^{\pi} [ a_n \cos(nx)\cos(kx) + b_n \sin(nx)\cos(kx)] \, dx
\end{align}
$$
根据三角函数系的正交性, 其他项在 $[-\pi, \pi]$上的积分均为0, 即:
$$
\begin{align}
& \int_{-\pi}^{\pi} f(x)\cos(kx) \, dx \\ 
=& \int_{-\pi}^{\pi} a_k \cos(kx)\cos(kx) \, dx \\
=& \int_{-\pi}^{\pi} \frac{a_k}{2} (1 + \cos(2kx)) \, dx \\
=& \frac{a_k}{2}x \Big|_{-\pi}^{\pi} + 0 \\
=& a_k \pi
\end{align}
$$
解得: $a_k = \frac{1}{\pi} \int_{-\pi}^{\pi} f(x)\cos(kx) \, dx$.

### 计算 $b_k, k \in [1, \dots , n]$ 
对等式两边同时乘以 $\sin (kx)$,并在区间 $[-\pi, \pi]$ 求积分:
$$
\begin{align}
& \int_{-\pi}^{\pi} f(x)\sin(kx) \, dx \\ 
=& 0 + \sum_{n=1}^{\infty} \int_{-\pi}^{\pi} [ a_n \cos(nx)\sin(kx) + b_n \sin(nx)\sin(kx)] \, dx
\end{align}
$$
根据三角函数系的正交性, 其他项在 $[-\pi, \pi]$上的积分均为0, 即:
$$
\begin{align}
& \int_{-\pi}^{\pi} f(x)\sin(kx) \, dx \\ 
=& \int_{-\pi}^{\pi} b_k \sin(kx)\sin(kx) \, dx \\
=& \int_{-\pi}^{\pi} \frac{b_k}{2} (1 - \cos(2kx)) \, dx \\
=& \frac{b_k}{2}x \Big|_{-\pi}^{\pi} - 0 \\
=& b_k \pi
\end{align}
$$
解得: $b_k = \frac{1}{\pi} \int_{-\pi}^{\pi} f(x)\sin(kx) \, dx$. 

## 傅里叶级数的收敛性
### Riemann 引理
设 $f \in R[a, b]$, 则:
$$ \lim_{\lambda \to +\infty} \int_a^b f(x) \sin(\lambda x) \, dx = 0 $$
$$ \lim_{\lambda \to +\infty} \int_a^b f(x) \cos(\lambda x) \, dx = 0 $$
### 证明 $\lim_{\lambda \to +\infty} \int_a^b f(x) \sin(\lambda x) \, dx = 0$
$$
\begin{align}
& \int_a^b f(x) \sin(\lambda x) \, dx \\
=& \int_a^b f(x) \, d\frac{\sin(\lambda x)}{\lambda} \\
=& \frac{f(x) \sin (\lambda x)}{\lambda} \Big|_{a}^{b} - \int_a^b \frac{\sin (\lambda x)}{\lambda} f'(x) \, dx
\end{align}
$$
两边同时取绝对值:
$$
\begin{align}
& \left | \int_a^b f(x) \sin(\lambda x) \, dx \right | \\
=& \left | \frac{f(x) \sin (\lambda x)}{\lambda} \Big|_{a}^{b} - \int_a^b \frac{\sin (\lambda x)}{\lambda} f'(x) \, dx \right | \\
\le & \left | \frac{f(x) \sin (\lambda x)}{\lambda} \Big|_{a}^{b}  \right | + \left | \int_a^b \frac{\sin (\lambda x)}{\lambda} f'(x) \, dx \right | \\
\end{align}
$$
因为 $\sin(\lambda x) \in [-1, 1]$: 
$$
\begin{align}
& \left | \int_a^b f(x) \sin(\lambda x) \, dx \right | \\
\le & \left | \frac{f(x) \sin (\lambda x)}{\lambda} \Big|_{a}^{b}  \right | + \left | \int_a^b \frac{\sin (\lambda x)}{\lambda} f'(x) \, dx \right | \\
\le & \left | \frac{f(x)}{\lambda} \Big|_{a}^{b}  \right | + \left | \int_a^b \frac{1}{\lambda} f'(x) \, dx \right | \\
\le & \frac{ \left | f(b) \right |}{\lambda} + \frac{ \left | f(a) \right |}{\lambda} + \frac{1}{\lambda} \left | \int_a^b  f'(x) \, dx \right | \\
\end{align}
$$
若 $f(x)$ 在 $[a, b]$ 上连续可微, 
$$
f(b), f(a), \int_a^b  f'(x) \, dx 
$$
 的值均有界, 因此当 $\lambda$ 趋近于无穷大时:
 $$
\begin{align}
& \lim_{\lambda \to +\infty} \left | \int_a^b f(x) \sin(\lambda x) \, dx \right | \\
\le & \lim_{\lambda \to +\infty} \frac{ \left | f(b) \right |}{\lambda} + \lim_{\lambda \to +\infty} \frac{ \left | f(a) \right |}{\lambda} + \lim_{\lambda \to +\infty} \frac{1}{\lambda} \left | \int_a^b  f'(x) \, dx \right | \\
=& 0
\end{align}
$$
同理可证: $\lim_{\lambda \to +\infty} \int_a^b f(x) \cos(\lambda x) \, dx = 0$
